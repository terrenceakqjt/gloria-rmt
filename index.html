About Your Therapist

Gloria is a Registered Massage Therapist working out of the clinic, Kinetic Evolution in Kelowna, BC. She previously worked at 2 clinics full-time in Vancouver, but found the quieter life in Kelowna compelling and moved over with her dog in September, 2026.

Whether you're dealing with chronic tension, recovering from something, or just need an hour to slow down and breathe, Gloria will be glad to welcome you into her care.
