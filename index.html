A bit about me

I'm a Registered Massage Therapist at Hartwell Therapy & Wellness, and I bring my background in neurobiology and anatomy from the University of Otago into every session I do. I trained at VCMT, but honestly, what matters most to me is listening — figuring out what your body actually needs instead of running through a script.

Whether you're dealing with chronic tension, recovering from something, or just need an hour to slow down and breathe, I'm glad to have you in my care.
