About

A science-based approach to bodywork

Registered Massage Therapist at Hartwell Therapy & Wellness, integrating bodywork with a foundation in neurobiology and anatomy from the University of Otago. Drawing on training from VCMT, I focus on client-centered care that bridges scientific knowledge with hands-on practice — helping clients move better, feel better, and stay well.

Every treatment plan is tailored to the person in front of me: whether that's easing chronic tension, supporting recovery, or simply making space to slow down.
